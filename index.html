Examiner Notes:
  • Dijkstra's Algorithm (Min-Heap): Guarantees the absolute minimum distance on non-negative weighted graphs (O((V + E) log V)).
  • A* Search: Uses Haversine Euclidean distance heuristic h(n) to direct exploration toward target, visiting fewer total nodes.
  • BFS: Guarantees minimum number of intermediate road intersections/hops, but does not consider edge physical length in meters.
  • DFS: Traverses deeply along path branches; fast path discovery but produces non-optimal physical walking distances.
